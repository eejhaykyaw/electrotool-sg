Engineering workflow

  1. Define scope: drawings, specifications, site conditions and supply parameters.
  2. Develop design: loads, distribution hierarchy, cable routes and protection studies.
  3. Quantify: material takeoff, BOQ, SOR and commercial assumptions.
  4. Verify: standards, technical submittals, calculations and authority requirements.
  5. Deliver: approved drawings, installation records, test results and handover.
Engineering-use notice: Preliminary calculation and estimating tools only. Verify design, quantities, supplier rates, statutory requirements and technical submissions against current Singapore standards, project specifications, manufacturer data and competent professional review.

Cable & voltage-drop calculator

Calculate voltage drop from a user-supplied cable resistance/reactance or voltage-drop constant.

Voltage drop – impedance method

Enter values and calculate.

Uses ΔV = √3 × I × L × (R cosφ + X sinφ) / 1000 for three-phase, and 2 × I × L × (...) / 1000 for single-phase. R and X are per conductor in Ω/km; length is one-way. Assumes steady-state sinusoidal conditions.

What you still need to verify

  • Current-carrying capacity for the actual installation method.
  • Ambient temperature, grouping and thermal insulation derating.
  • Conductor material, insulation temperature rating and termination limits.
  • Short-circuit withstand and automatic disconnection requirements.
  • Protective-device coordination and maximum permitted voltage drop.
  • Manufacturer cable data and current Singapore standards.

SOR rate calculator

Composite unit-rate build-up for labour, materials, equipment and overhead.

Rate methodology

Direct cost = material + labour + equipment + other direct cost. Overhead is applied to direct cost; profit is applied to direct cost plus overhead; tax is applied last if applicable.

Contract SOR rules may use a different markup sequence. Confirm the contract's commercial methodology and tax treatment.
